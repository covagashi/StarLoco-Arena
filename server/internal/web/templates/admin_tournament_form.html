Schedule

Registration closes when the first search period starts. In each period an entrant presses Combattre to meet the coach facing them; whoever was searching when the period closes wins an unplayed fixture by forfeit. The tournament finishes when it has a winner or its last period closes. Leave the start empty for a tournament that is always open and never finishes.
